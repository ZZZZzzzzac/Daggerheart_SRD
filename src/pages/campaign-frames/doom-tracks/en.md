You can use a Doom Track to represent the growing disasters
that might befall your group’s setting if the party acts too
slowly. You can make your own Doom Track using the example
track available in Hope & Fear or at daggerheart.com/
downloads for inspiration.
To use a Doom Track, mark a box in the party’s tier or below at
the end of each session. You can’t mark a nested box until you
mark the box it’s nested under. As your table’s story develops,
you can adjust how many boxes you mark at the end of a
session.
